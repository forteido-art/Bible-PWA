App Instructions

🚀 Getting Started

When you first open the app, it tries to load a bundled Bible translation automatically. If none is found, you will see an upload screen.

  • Tap Choose JSON File and select a valid Bible JSON (structured books → chapters → verses, or a flat list of verses). The file is stored locally so it loads instantly next time.
  • Once loaded, the Read tab becomes your main reading view. Book and chapter dropdowns appear in the header.
  • Your last book, chapter, and scroll position are remembered automatically—even after closing the app or putting the device to sleep.

📖 Reading the Bible

The reader shows one chapter at a time with clear verse numbers and readable text.

  • Book & chapter selectors — Use the two pills in the top bar to jump to any book or chapter.
  • Swipe to change chapter — On the reading screen, swipe left to go to the next chapter, or swipe right to go to the previous chapter. Swipes work across book boundaries (last chapter of a book → first of the next, and vice versa). At the very beginning or end of the Bible you will see a short toast message.
  • Auto-hiding bars — Scroll down and the header and bottom navigation slide away so you get a full-screen reading experience. Tap once anywhere on the page (or on a verse) to bring them back. The last verse you were looking at stays visible.
  • Tap a verse that already has a note — Opens that note in a quick view drawer so you can read your reflection without hunting for it.

📋 Quick Verse Copy

Tap directly on any verse number (the small blue number at the start of the verse). The full reference and text (e.g. “John 3:16 – For God so loved…”) is copied to your clipboard instantly, and a toast confirms the copy.

⚙️ Long-Press Menu (Verse Actions)

Press and hold on the body text of any verse (not the number). After a short moment the verse highlights and a bottom action sheet appears with these options:

  • Multi-verse copy — Enters selection mode. Tap any verses you want (they highlight). Use the bottom bar to Copy Selected or Cancel. Copied text is neatly grouped by book and chapter with smart ranges (e.g. “Genesis 1:1-5, 8”).
  • Add note / Edit note — Opens a notepad drawer linked to that exact verse. Type freely; notes autosave. Verses that have a note get a subtle left border so you can spot them later. Tapping a noted verse opens the note in view mode; use Edit to change it.
  • Highlight text — Cycles through yellow → green → blue → pink → none. Highlights are saved and restored every time you open that chapter.
  • Underline verse — Toggles a clear underline on the verse for quick visual emphasis. Also saved permanently.
  • Search dictionary — Opens an external dictionary / study resource in a new browser tab so you can look up words or the passage.
  • Cross references — Opens a bottom sheet of related verses (OpenBible data). See the dedicated section below for full details.
  • Strong's concordance — Opens the verse with every word that carries a Strong's number underlined and tappable. See the Strong's section below.

Tap outside the menu or tap the same verse again to dismiss it.

📖 Strong's Concordance

From the long-press menu choose Strong's concordance. A sheet shows the full verse text.

  • Words that have a Strong's number are underlined in the primary colour.
  • Tap any underlined word — Opens a full offline dictionary card with: Strong's number (H… Hebrew / G… Greek), original-language spelling, transliteration, definition, KJV English meanings, the English word as it appears in this verse, and any Tense/Voice/Mood markers.
  • Requires kjv_strongs.json (verse markup) and strongs_dict.json (Hebrew & Greek lexicon) next to the app, just like crossrefs.json. No internet needed.

🔗 Cross References

From the long-press menu choose Cross references. A sheet lists parallel and related verses ranked by strength (stars).

  • Single tap a result — Expands or collapses the full verse text in place so you can read it without leaving the list.
  • Double-tap a result — Jumps straight to that verse in the main reader and briefly highlights it.
  • Long-press a result — Copies that single verse (reference + text) to the clipboard.
  • Multi-select icon (copy-check) at the top — Lets you select several cross-references and copy them all at once. A footer shows the count and Copy / Cancel buttons.
  • Sticky-note icon — Opens the independent Notepad tab without closing the sheet first.
  • Close the sheet with the X or by tapping the dimmed area outside it.

🔍 Search

Switch to the Search tab at the bottom.

  • Type a word or phrase (at least 3 characters) and tap Search or press Enter.
  • Results show every matching verse across the whole Bible. Matching words are highlighted in yellow inside the preview.
  • Tap any result to jump to that exact verse in the reader; the verse is briefly highlighted so you can find it easily.
  • The search box stays fixed at the top while you scroll results. Clearing the search box resets the scroll position for next time.

📝 Notepad (Independent Notes)

The Notepad tab is a free-form jotter separate from verse-linked notes.

  • New Note — Creates a blank note with a title field and body. Notes autosave while you type (when in edit mode).
  • Tap a card in the list to open it in view mode. Use the pencil icon to edit, the trash icon to delete (with confirmation), or the PDF icon to export / share.
  • Share as PDF — Generates a clean PDF of the note. On supported devices the system share sheet appears so you can send it; otherwise the file downloads.
  • Empty new notes that you never typed in are discarded automatically when you go back to the list.
  • All notepad entries are stored on your device and survive app restarts.

⚙️ Settings

  • How to Use App Functions — Opens this guide (you are here). Use the back link at the top to return to Settings.
  • Clear Database — Permanently removes the loaded Bible translation and resets highlights, verse notes, underlines, and reading position. You will need to load a JSON file again (or let the app try the bundled file). Use this when you want to switch to a different translation.

💡 Tips & Little Details

  • Everything important (highlights, underlines, verse notes, independent notepad entries, last reading place) is saved locally on your device—no account required.
  • The green Donate button in the header opens a support page in your browser if you wish to contribute.
  • When the app is embedded inside another portal, layout automatically adjusts so nothing is clipped.
  • If cross-reference data is missing (offline or not bundled), the sheet will simply say the data is unavailable; the rest of the app continues to work normally.
