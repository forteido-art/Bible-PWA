App Instructions

📚 Quick Verse Copy

Simply tap directly on any verse number to copy its contents alongside the Book, Chapter, and Verse reference to your clipboard instantly.

⚙️ Long Press Menu Features

Press and hold on the body text of any verse to reveal advanced contextual actions:

  • Multi-verse copy: Select and copy multiple verses at once. Tapped verses will be appended together under a unified book and chapter header reference.
  • Highlight text: Cycle through colorful background highlight markers.
  • Underline verse: Underline key study materials directly in real-time.
  • Add / Edit Notes: Open an automatic draft notepad drawer to link local reflections. Tapping a verse that has a note icon on it will instantly view your note.
  • Search Dictionary: Link the verse to external language studies via external references.

🔍 Global Index Search

Toggle the Search icon in your navigation deck to query matching occurrences instantly across the entire Bible library.
